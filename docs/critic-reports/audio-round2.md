## What is wrong (ranked)

1. **[minor] The master compressor is tuned as a compressor, not a limiter, and it is eating the piano** — `synth.ts:217-221`: threshold −6 dB, knee 10 dB, ratio 3. Gain reduction begins at −16 dBFS, so it acts on ordinary single-voice material (a solo-track render peaking at −10.5 dBFS is inside the knee). Measured consequences: the crest factor of the mix is 13.4 dB (a struck piano is 18–20 dB), the +5 dB the builder added at the synth reached the output as +1.7 dB, the onset heuristic dropped from 7 to 6 detected onsets, the retrigger dip on strings is filled in (issue 4), and the mix still sits at −22 dBFS RMS / −8.6 peak at velocity 0.8. Fix: make it a true safety limiter (threshold −2 to −1 dB, knee 0–3 dB, ratio ≥ 12, attack 1–3 ms, release 50–100 ms) and set the gain structure so a four-voice velocity-0.8 chord peaks about −3 dBFS pre-limiter (raise `velocityToPeak` / `peakMax` or the default master accordingly). Expect peaks near −3 dBFS, RMS around −17/−18, transients intact. Verify with the crest factor and the onset count, not just RMS.
2. **[minor] `load()` hard-cuts audio at the engine API** — `engine.ts:521-525`: `releaseAll(now)` schedules a 35 ms fade, then `teardownTrackNodes()` disconnects the track buses synchronously, so the fade never reaches the output. Live capture: the master envelope goes 0.12 → 0 within one 10 ms window (max step 0.041, up to the instantaneous sample value on other phases). Same for `stop(); load()` called back-to-back. Unreachable through the controller (which stops during 'loading' and loads after parsing) but any direct caller will hear a tick. Fix: keep the old buses alive until their voices have released (e.g. disconnect them on a 300 ms timer or when `voices` referencing them are gone), or release voices directly into the master and only then disconnect.
3. **[minor] `Voice.cancel()` leaves the oscillators running** — `synth.ts:196-202` zeroes and disconnects the envelope but never calls `restop(now)`, so a cancelled pending 3-qn note keeps four oscillators and their gains rendering silently until the original stop time (seconds at slow tempi). Seek/tempo storms over long notes accumulate them. Fix: `restop(Math.max(ctx.currentTime, t0) + 0.001)` inside `cancel()`.
4. **[minor] Same-pitch retriggers on the sustained timbre barely re-articulate** — the 25 ms choke lead with a 35 ms time constant (`envelope.ts:59-61`) leaves the old voice at 49 % of its level at the new onset while the new voice is still in its 49–60 ms attack, and the compressor's 150 ms release fills the dip; measured on the strings render the trough is 0.058 against a 0.065 sustain (89 %) and the retriggered note settles at the same level as the previous sustain. Musically a repeated note on strings reads as one long note with a wobble. Fix: lead ≈ 45 ms with tau ≈ 15 ms so the dip reaches ≤ 25 %, and/or give retriggers a short accent (×1.15 peak, faster attack), and re-measure after issue 1 removes the compressor's fill-in.
5. **[minor] `finish()` forgets ringing voices** — `engine.ts:409-417` clears `voices` and `lastByMidi` at the end of the score without releasing, so an immediate `play()` restart re-strikes pitches over their still-ringing predecessors without the retrigger choke, and `load()` right after the end disconnects buses under ringing voices (issue 2). Harmless with the demo (0.052 max step on restart), but keep the voices tracked until their oscillators end (they already call `onEnded`) instead of clearing the map.
6. **[minor] Showcase cosmetic** — the playhead marker and the orange "repeat" label collide at the repeat boundary (`showcase.ts:186-190`, screenshot 05). Draw the label below the marker row or hide it while the playhead is within a few pixels.
7. **[minor] `play()` resolves silently when the context cannot start** — the caller gets `undefined` whether playback started or `resumeContext()` timed out; the only signal is `getState().playing === false` with `contextState 'suspended'`. Fine for the contract, but a UI wanting to show "tap to enable audio" has to infer it. Consider a change request adding a `lastError`/`blocked` hint to `TransportState` or letting `play()` resolve to a boolean.
